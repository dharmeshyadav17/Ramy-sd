About

Since 2016, cooking the long way round.

Remy D'Souza opened a nine-table trattoria in Bandra before bringing the kitchen to Gurugram. The rule hasn't changed: nothing on the pass comes out of a packet. Dough ferments for 48 hours, ragù cooks for six, and the focaccia is stretched by hand at 7 AM.

Today Remy's is a 120-cover room at Magnum Global Park with a terrace, a bar that runs the length of the dining room, and a private cellar for the nights that need their own door.
